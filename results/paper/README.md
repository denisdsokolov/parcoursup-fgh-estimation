# Manuscript results

These compact tables are the public numeric record transcribed from the manuscript rather than
recomputed from the historical CSVs in `../simulations/`.

- `mechanism_comparison.csv`: Tables 5, D.9, and D.13 for 2024, 2022, and 2023. Standard errors
  are Monte Carlo standard errors; differences are paired `SF-CDA - PCS`.
- `preference_estimates.csv`: selected coefficients and sample dimensions from the annual
  preference-estimation tables, including Table D.14 for 2024.

Blank `observed` entries denote outcomes that require a simulated preference profile and hence
have no directly observed counterpart. Significance markers follow the paper.
